              Welcome to lockedin

              The focus timer you leave running while you work.

              • ◷
                Track your timeHit Lock in, then work in any other tab or app.
              • ◉
                Catch your phoneThe camera notices your phone, dozing off, and zoning out, and nudges you back. No setup needed.
              • ✓
                Plan and reviewSet goals for the day and see when you focus best.

              Private by design

              The phone check runs entirely on your device. No video or photo ever leaves your computer, and nothing is recorded. The camera is optional, and the timer works without it.

              There's no setup. lockedin learns how you sit during your first few seconds and keeps adjusting as you work.

              How lockedin works

              Leave it running

              Start a session, then work anywhere else on your computer. The tab shows your live time, and you'll get a notification if you pick up your phone or it's time for a break. On Chrome and Edge, the pop-out button opens a small floating timer that stays on top of your other windows.

              How the phone check decides

              • On your phone: it sees a phone, or your head stays tilted down for a few seconds.
              • Away: nobody is in front of the camera.
              • Writing on paper? Turn on Paper mode in Settings so looking down doesn't count. Only an actual phone will.

              Your data

              Everything stays in this browser. There are no accounts. AI planning sends only the text you type, your goal titles and simple stats (hours, focus score) to generate a plan, and nothing is stored.

              Phone catcher

              Paper mode ignores looking down, so only a visible phone counts. Good for writing or reading on your desk. Chatting is noticed from mouth movement only; the microphone is never used. Turn on group work for study groups or team projects so talking counts as focus.
